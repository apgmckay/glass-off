Glass Off

Evening session, flat lake, rope tight. Carve the wake and send it.

  • Hold left or right (or arrow keys) to dig an edge. On a phone you can switch on Tilt and carve by leaning.
  • Edge away from the boat to load the rope, cut back hard, and hit the lip fast to pop. Hold a side in the air to spin.
  • In the air, swipe for flips and board tricks, tap to grab. Finish them before you land.
  • Launch off kickers. Line up with rails to grind and lean against the balance bar.
  • Three falls and the session's over.
